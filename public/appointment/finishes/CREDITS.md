# Studio finishes

Masonry, timber and honed stone are locally served, resized CC0 derivatives already prepared for this portfolio. Sources are Poly Haven’s [Sandstone Blocks 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_05), [Wood Table 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001) and [Concrete Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02). The masonry photograph is graded to suggest the iron-rich colour of laterite.

The basalt, asphalt and limewash maps are original procedural finishes. The courtyard tree, branching leaf atlas, building and furnishings are generated locally. The approximate furniture dimensions and shared primitives come from the portfolio’s existing studio scene.

The two seated figures reuse the existing studio cast, with CC0 MakeHuman-derived facial geometry. See [the cast asset source and dedication](../../../src/studio/assets/CAST-LICENSE.md).
