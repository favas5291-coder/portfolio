The two facial surfaces and scalp caps in `cast-face.bin` are local derivatives of the MakeHuman HM08 base mesh and its bundled macrodetail shapes, reduced and cropped for this scene. The source asset carries a CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

Source: [MakeHuman base mesh](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj) · [Asset dedication](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/LICENSE.ASSETS.md).

The original cast design, clothing, props, poses and performance are authored in `officePeople.js`. The avatars represent illustrative roles in a virtual studio.
