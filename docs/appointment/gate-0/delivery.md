# Delivering the two views

## What the silent visitor sees

At 390 × 844, use a shallow, independently framed architectural view above ordinary HTML. Keep the strip of trim at the lower edge of the image. Put the name and role, offer, a recognizable landscape GymDrobe capture with its link, “Chat on WhatsApp” and the single scroll cue in the first viewport. Reserve this space in the prerender. Give the name more prominence than the chapter title. A returning visitor can jump to the work or the desk through the existing navigation. At 1440 px, give the architecture the larger side of a split view. The phone composition and the architectural aperture can change independently of text scaling.

At the desk, hold the visitor beside the guest chair with a clear view of the window jamb, open timber bay, kerb and both near wheels. Keep the 0.75 m desk and monitor to one side of that view. The monitor displays the last inspected project from the preview manifest. The HTML asks “What are you planning to build?” and opens an editable WhatsApp draft. Focus and typing never advance the camera.

## Recommended hybrid

1. **Author once.** Build the car with its working passenger hinge, shell, canopy and tree in metres in Blender. Make a single surveyed camera and door rig the source for both still cameras and browser playback. Set all stationary opening and closing poses explicitly. Check the section with roof trim, seals, seat, frame, final-depth eaves and desk envelope present.
2. **Prove one live transition locally.** Export the visible assets as authored GLBs, bake static indirect light and contact occlusion, instance foliage and use live shadows for the two doors. Keep the background subdued and light the room beyond the open timber panel. For the look test, set late-afternoon sun at azimuth 248° clockwise from north and elevation 16°. This throws a long shadow from the courtyard tree across the plaster; keep the sun and environment rotation fixed through the visit. Compare wet and sheltered samples in the same frame. Try ACES, SSAO and bloom individually with GPU time and text contrast visible.
3. **Make the first frame independent of the GPU.** Export compact and wide stills from the same rig. Load one current-size opening still with the useful HTML. Start one canvas on exploration intent, read current normalized scroll progress at takeover and wait for the first complete frame. Keep it on demand when the view settles. Fetch the upper floor on approach. Reduced motion and failure use composed stills and the same HTML navigation.
4. **Decide motion media on evidence.** Gate 1 starts with live GLBs and matched stills. If close-up materials or motion at the threshold fall short after correction, present a matched Cycles frame of the same pose. After visual approval, compare short segments of independently framed AVIF/WebP frames and short-GOP video. Seek from normalized progress in both directions, cancel obsolete requests on flings, and hold the last decoded frame until the desired frame is ready. Record actual transfer, decode stalls and reversal latency on the phone viewport. A sequence cost quoted before an encode would be speculative.
5. **Keep screens replaceable.** Export blank monitor plates, their four projected corners and an occlusion matte for any foreground object crossing the glass. Fit each registered capture at its own aspect ratio inside that plate. For moving footage, export corners and occlusion per frame. A subsequent preview refresh must update the still, live view and footage without a new architecture render. At inspection, stop motion and use a selectable, focusable HTML view with a native-size enlargement.

The three-way decision follows the actual prior measurements. Full real-time delivery puts the most expensive foliage, reflections and close-up materials on the weakest device. Full pre-rendering pays in downloaded frames and reverse seeking and still needs live compositing for the chosen project. The hybrid provides immediate contact and work samples and gives the hard shot a measurable path to either representation. PR #18's recorded median mobile lab score was 91, but its moving-frame p95 was 333 ms on emulated phone with SwiftShader and its LCP was 3.51 s. None of those numbers estimates this proposed build on physical hardware. ## Gate 1 decision material

Use matching initial and final frames at 390 and 1440 px, with the same world-space car, hinge, kerb, canopy and window. Inspect full-size crops of the latch recess, rubber seal, wall reveal, branch fork and wet-to-dry transition. Record an uncut phone-viewport exit, reversal while crossing the sill, and resumption on the pavement. Include the shot with and without clearance guides. Sweep a head-sized volume against roof, door and kerb along the entire reversible path as well as testing near-plane clipping. Render the final desk and monitor *envelopes* as occluders during the sightline check. These are proof requirements for the next local gate.
