## Hero model briefs

Dimensions below are design constraints in metres. The accompanying plan governs pivots, positioning and floor levels.

- **Passenger assembly.** The proposed compact hatch measures 4.30 × 1.76 m, with a 2.72 m wheelbase and an outer roof about 1.51 m above road and a clear side-glass aperture to about 1.43 m. Project the mirror into the frame with its nearest visible edge at (7.58, −5.47, +1.05). Model the left front door, 1.45 m hinge-to-tail with a 68° swing. The car stands 0.32 m from a 0.14 m kerb. Finish the continuous inner capping with a compressed rubber lip, a return around the sill, an inset latch bowl with thickness, and glass seated in its channel. Supply a passenger-eye view, hinge-end view, underside at maximum swing, exterior side and upper three-quarter views. Require plausible specular movement on trim as the door reverses and recognizable wheel openings from upstairs.
- **Shell.** Hold a 10.20 × 7.25 m envelope with an inset court and a 0.32 m front wall. The upstairs floor sits at +3.40 m. The 2.44 m fixed safety glazing runs from +3.66 to +5.57 m with a 1.29 m opening in the timber screen. At the offset entrance the canopy measures 2.20 × 1.80 m with a soffit at +2.90 m. Author front elevation, wall/soffit section, window-jamb return seen from both sides and a close view through the entry. Give the sheltered paving visibly higher roughness than the irregular wet runnels outside. Use orange only on the hardware and lettering. Test both traced views with the façade, door, desk, chair and monitor in their intended positions.
- **Rain tree.** Place a 0.42 m diameter trunk at plan (1.72, 1.83). Shape the selected limb to reach plan (6.00, −1.15) at +4.50 m. Model continuous bark ridges into the crotch, taper beyond the landing, stagger fine twigs and use paired leaflets that break up the exterior shadow. Inspect at the landing, at the root flare and from the passenger seat with the opening visible. Check the crown against the eave and office glazing in plan and section.

## Authoring and client input

This workspace ran Blender 4.3.2 headlessly with Cycles enabled and the glTF export operator present. FFmpeg lists AVIF, WebP and MP4 muxers and AV1, WebP and H.264 encoders. A Khronos KTX/Basis encoder was absent from PATH and the checked local tool directories. Set up and verify the encoder before claiming compressed-texture delivery. Inspect UV seams, normal orientation, pivots and compressed texture response at the closest camera distance. Bake the static set once, then use the same scene for the fallback plates and the live renderer.

The original-design route requires no client-supplied model, paid licence or portrait to start Gate 1. A particular real car, real office or named person's likeness would require the corresponding rights-cleared views and dimensions if requested. The present brief supports a virtual office and a generic original vehicle. Update this register with actual author credits, versioned source locations, modifications and licence evidence when each asset enters the build. Client input at this gate is the architectural direction decision.
