## Five-second review

Give a business owner a silent phone. Show the opening for five seconds, then ask who the developer is, what he builds and how they would contact him. Let them find GymDrobe without coaching. At the desk, ask what they saw Favas build that made them confident enough to contact him. Record their actual words and the first point at which they needed help.
